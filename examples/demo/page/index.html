Watch a stop hook send broken code back

When a coding agent ends its turn, stop-rules cuts what it changed into pieces and asks a judge one yes or no question per piece and rule. A score of 0.6 or more means the rule is broken, and that piece goes back to the agent to fix before you see it. Pick a judge to replay the same two turns with its own measured speed, cost and catch rate.
